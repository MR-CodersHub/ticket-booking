Our Journey

Seven Years of
Extraordinary Events

From a small startup to a global platform, every step of our journey has been driven by passion for connecting people with the moments that matter most.

2019
Eventora Founded
Born in a small co-working space with a vision to democratize event ticketing. Launched beta with 50 local events in New York.
2020
Virtual Events Pivot
Rapidly adapted to support virtual and hybrid events, onboarding 10,000+ users during the global shift to online experiences.
2021
Series A Funding & 100K Users
Secured $12M in Series A funding and crossed the 100,000 user milestone. Expanded to 50+ cities across North America.
2022
Global Expansion
Launched in Europe and Asia-Pacific markets. Partnered with 500+ event organizers and reached 500K registered users.
2024
1 Million Tickets Milestone
Celebrated selling our millionth ticket. Launched the AI-powered event recommendation engine and mobile app v3.0.
2026
2M+ Users & 15 Countries
Today, Eventora powers 50K+ events across 500 cities in 15 countries, with 2 million passionate users and growing every day.

What We Stand For

Our Mission & Values

These six principles guide every decision we make — from the features we build to the partnerships we form.

Innovation
We continuously push boundaries, using cutting-edge technology to reimagine how people discover and experience events.
Trust
Secure payments, verified organizers, and transparent policies ensure every transaction is safe and reliable.
Inclusivity
Events are for everyone. We celebrate diversity and work to ensure our platform is accessible to all communities.
Excellence
From user experience to customer support, we hold ourselves to the highest standards in every interaction.
Community
We're more than a platform — we're a community of event lovers, organizers, and creators building something bigger together.
Impact
Every event creates ripples — economic, cultural, and social. We amplify those ripples to build a better world.
