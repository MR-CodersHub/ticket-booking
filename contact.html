FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about Eventora's platform, support, and policies.

Creating an account is free and takes less than 2 minutes. Click "Get Started" in the top navigation bar, enter your email address, choose a secure password, and verify your email. You can also sign up with Google or Apple ID for a faster experience. Once verified, your dashboard will be ready to explore immediately.

Our support team responds to all email inquiries within 24 business hours. For urgent technical issues, we recommend using our Live Chat feature for near-instant responses (available Mon–Fri, 9AM–8PM EST). Partnership and media inquiries are typically handled within 48 hours by the relevant team.

First, check your registered email inbox (including Spam/Promotions folders) for your ticket confirmation. Log into your account and visit My Tickets in the dashboard. If your ticket still doesn't appear, contact our support team via Live Chat or email with your order confirmation number and we'll resolve it immediately — often within minutes.

Yes! The Eventora mobile app is available on both the Apple App Store and Google Play Store. The app offers the full ticketing experience including digital wallets, event reminders, in-app navigation, and exclusive app-only deals. We recommend keeping the app updated for the best experience.

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and cryptocurrency (Bitcoin, Ethereum) on select events. For corporate accounts, we also offer invoice-based billing. All transactions are secured with 256-bit SSL encryption.

Refund policies vary by event and are set by individual organizers. Most events offer full refunds if cancelled more than 72 hours in advance. If an event is cancelled by the organizer, all ticket holders receive automatic full refunds within 5–7 business days. Eventora's service fees are non-refundable unless the event is organizer-cancelled. See our Refund Policy for complete details.

We love partnering with venues, promoters, and event organizers of all sizes. To apply, fill out our contact form selecting "Partnerships" as the subject, or email us directly at partnerships@eventora.com. Our partnerships team will review your application and schedule a discovery call within 3 business days. We offer tiered partnership plans from indie venues to major arenas.

Yes! Eventora provides a comprehensive REST API that allows partners to integrate ticketing, inventory management, and analytics directly into their own platforms. Our API supports webhooks, OAuth 2.0 authentication, and real-time event data. Access our Developer Documentation or contact our partnerships team to request API credentials and sandbox access.
