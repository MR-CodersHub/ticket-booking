Your Trusted Booking Partner

Eventora's Ticket Booking Assistance service revolutionizes how you secure your spot at the world's most exciting events. Whether it's a sold-out concert, a championship sports match, a business conference, or a cultural festival — our platform ensures you never miss out on the moments that matter.

Our intelligent booking engine scans thousands of event listings in real-time, offering you the best available seats at competitive prices. With our secure payment gateway and instant digital ticket delivery, the entire process from discovery to entry-ready takes under 3 minutes.

We go beyond simple transactions. Our concierge-level support team is on call around the clock to assist with seat selection, group bookings, accessibility needs, and any last-minute changes. Your experience is our priority — before, during, and after the event.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our ticket booking service.

Your digital ticket is delivered instantly upon successful payment confirmation. You'll receive it via email, SMS, and within your Eventora account dashboard — typically within 30 seconds of completing the transaction.

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), digital wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal), UPI, net banking from 50+ banks, and EMI options for bookings above $200. All transactions are fully secured.

Yes! Cancellations are accepted up to 24 hours before the event for a full refund. Exchanges are available up to 48 hours before the event (subject to availability). Some events may have different policies — these are clearly displayed before checkout.

Groups of 10 or more qualify for special discounts of 10–25%. Contact our group sales team directly, and they'll provide a dedicated booking manager, reserved block seating, and a custom invoice for corporate billing. We've managed groups of up to 2,000 attendees.

If an event is cancelled, you'll receive a 100% automatic refund within 5–7 business days. If postponed, we'll offer you the choice to keep your tickets for the new date or receive a full refund. We monitor event statuses 24/7 and notify you immediately via email and SMS.

Eventora charges a transparent service fee of 2–5% per booking (varies by event type). This fee covers platform maintenance, payment processing, and 24/7 support. The exact fee is always displayed on the checkout page before you confirm payment — no hidden charges ever.

Yes, ticket transfers are supported for most events. Log into your Eventora account, navigate to "My Tickets," and use the "Transfer Ticket" option. The recipient will receive a new QR code immediately. Some events disable transfers for security reasons.

We offer wheelchair-accessible seating, companion seat allocation, hearing loop sections, and large-print ticket options. Our accessibility team can be reached 24/7 to arrange any special requirements. Simply select "Accessibility Needs" during checkout and we'll handle the rest.
